### Lessons
- A process-wide `Once` scrub is wrong for any test binary that calls a command's `run()` in-process. It also removes the forced opt-outs (`SOCKET_NO_CONFIG`, `SOCKET_TELEMETRY_DISABLED`) that `run()` reads. Keep those opt-outs and force them on.
- A `Once` scrub never runs again. Every `set_var` in such a binary has to go through a helper that removes the var before any assertion, or a failing assertion leaks the var into later tests.
