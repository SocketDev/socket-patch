### Chosen
`remove <target>` and `rollback <target>` say "No patch found" when the vendor ledger cannot be read (issue #2249). I chose it because the user is told the wrong thing about the one file that records a vendored project's patches, a merge conflict in `.socket/vendor/state.json` is enough to cause it, and the contract already says what is right: `not_found` and `patch_not_found` mean no manifest entry, ledger entry or hosted pin matches, and `report` fails with the ledger's error in the same case.

I found it by running the built binary over five small broken projects and comparing the commands, not from a lead:

```
$ socket-patch remove pkg:pypi/six@1.16.0
Error: No patch found matching identifier: pkg:pypi/six@1.16.0
$ socket-patch report pkg:pypi/six@1.16.0 --dry-run
Error: Could not read the vendor ledger: corrupt ./.socket/vendor/state.json: key must be a string at line 4 column 1. To report a patch anyway, name it by its UUID
```

The evidence is `a_target_is_never_not_found_while_the_vendor_ledger_is_unreadable` in `tests/manifest_load_error_matrix.rs`: 9 targeted commands over 3 projects. On `main`, `report` passes its 6 runs and `remove` / `rollback` fail their 21 with `not_found`, `patch_not_found` or `path_glob_no_match`.
