## False positives ruled out
- The control at first resolved the cache jar even with `mavenLocal()`. That's because Gradle's `mavenLocal()` ignores the `MAVEN_REPO_LOCAL` env var and honours only `-Dmaven.repo.local` / settings.xml. It was a harness artifact; with `-Dmaven.repo.local`, the control passes.
- `--offline` printCp failed once with "No cached version available" for commons-lang3, a partial warm-up from a Central hiccup. Re-ran online.
- `rollback` reported `partial_failure` only because the fixture didn't stage the before-blob offline. That's a fixture gap, not filed.
