- #535: `cli_properties` reads only `-Dk=v` tokens from `.mvn/maven.config`. `--define=k=v` (honoured by Maven 3.6.3–4.0.0-rc-7) and `-D k=v` (honoured by 3.6–3.9) are skipped, so a `${ct.version}` that maven.config raises to 1.11.0 is rewritten to `1.10.0-socket.*`: a silent downgrade, exit 0, no warning. The `-Dk=v` control warns `conflicting_literal_version` and keeps 1.11.0 on every line.
