- Vendored **`require-dev` entry in a project path with spaces + unicode** (`sp ü 漢/`): wired in `packages-dev`, installs patched, `--no-dev` skips it, `vex` attests from the committed artifact: **pass** on 1.10.28, 2.2.30 and 2.10.3.

Cargo restores it on the same path. Reproduced on 2.10.3 (×2) and 1.10.28. v4.0.0 behaves the same, so not a regression. Low impact: installs stay patched, and `vex` doesn't read the marker.
- 4 concurrent `vendor` runs: one wins, three get `lock_held`, and the lock and ledger come out consistent: **pass**.
- `vendor` SIGKILLed during the artifact download: only `.socket/apply.lock` is left (documented, reclaimed); the lock is untouched; a re-run vendors cleanly and installs patched: **pass**.

The same entry with a hand-added `dist.mirrors` + `transport-options.ssl`: the hosted rewrite removes `mirrors` (warned) and **keeps `transport-options`** (#399 family, already filed); rollback restores dist/source from packagist, so the hand-added `mirrors` isn't restored (documented `SOCKET_PACKAGIST_URL` upstream restore; packagist.org metadata carries no mirrors).

## False positives ruled out

- `repair` → `download_failed` "1 diff archive failed to download" on the agent-mode manifest: my mock lacked the `/patches/diff/<uuid>` route.
- `vendor --offline` → `vendor_service_offline_conflict`: vendored mode is service-only in v5 (documented in `--vendor-source`). Mock `patches/package` plus a single-top-dir zip instead.
- Hosted rollback not restoring hand-added `dist.mirrors` on a packagist-origin entry: restore comes from upstream packagist metadata (documented).
