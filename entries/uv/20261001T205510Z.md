The harness is new this run. It uses a Python mock patch API: the wheel is deterministic (fixed zip dates), `integrity.sha512` is in SRI form (`sha512-<b64>`, as the service sends it), and HEAD and `/blob/<git-sha256>` are served. `SOCKET_PYPI_JSON_API` points at a local pass-through to pypi.org (JSON only, with its URLs **not** rewritten, or rollback writes the forwarder's URLs into the lock). The pass-through also proxies `/simple/` with file URLs rewritten, so uv 0.0.5 can get past the sandbox TLS proxy (via `PUFFIN_INDEX_URL`). uv 0.1.x only needs `SSL_CERT_FILE`.
