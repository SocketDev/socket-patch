## False positives ruled out
- `vendor_fetch_failed` for files.pythonhosted.org during a lock-only repair is the sandbox TLS artifact (rustls vs the proxy CA). It passes in CI.
- The sidecar `socket-patch.vendor.json` being removed on a failed repair is the documented "nothing kept" contract. `vendor --revert` still restores the registry lock from state.json.
- Rollback with `.socket/vendor/redirect-state.json` deleted reports `Manifest not found`. The ledger is required by design.
- The Windows mock wheel sha differs from Linux/macOS (zlib output), which is a harness artifact, not a CLI bug.
