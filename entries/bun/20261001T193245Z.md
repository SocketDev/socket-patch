- **pass:** CRLF `bun.lock` (Windows `autocrlf` analog) on 1.1.45 v0, 1.3.14 and 1.4.2. Hosted scan keeps CRLF on every line, the frozen install is patched, and `rollback` is byte-exact for both `bun.lock` and the CRLF `package.json`. Vendored `vendor --revert` is byte-exact too and removes `.socket/`.
- **pass:** `bun install --yarn` projects (`bun.lockb` + `yarn.lock` on 1.1.45; `bun.lock` + `yarn.lock` on 1.2.23 and 1.4.2).

  - The lockb variant's `rollback` refuses with the `git checkout -- bun.lockb yarn.lock` remedy and touches neither file.
  - (Observation, not filed: on the text-lock variants `rollback` restores `bun.lock` byte-exact but rewrites Bun's `yarn.lock` `resolved` lines with a `#<sha1>` fragment Bun didn't write. Semantically equivalent, and it's yarn-classic territory.)

- **pass:** a Bun 1.1.45 binary workspace `bun.lockb` (root → a → b, two `is-number` versions).
  - Hosted rewrite, then cold frozen installs with 1.1.45, 1.2.23, 1.3.14 and 1.4.2: only the patched versions are patched.
