### False positives ruled out
- pnpm 10 `patch-commit` fails with `ERR_PNPM_LOCKFILE_CONFIG_MISMATCH` when `CI=true`, because CI implies a frozen install. Use `confirmModulesPurge=false` instead. It also needs the store dir the tree was installed with (`npm_config_store_dir`).
- A mock `/registry` serving a re-packed upstream tarball makes rollback restore a non-npmjs integrity. That's a fixture artifact.
- Release 4.0.0 `vex` exits 2 (`manifest_not_found`) on a v5 hosted checkout, because it has no ledger to read. That isn't a comparison point for GVS/vsd.
