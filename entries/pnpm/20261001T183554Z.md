### False positives ruled out
- pnpm 10 `configDependencies` copy (`node_modules/.pnpm-config/<pkg>`) stays unpatched under hosted mode, while `vex` attests `not_affected` for the regular copy. Config deps run only at install time (pnpmfile / config) and aren't part of the product's runtime graph, and on pnpm 10 their integrity lives in `pnpm-workspace.yaml`. Not filed.
- Hosted rollback keeps `trustLockfile: true` in a pre-existing workspace file with `pnpm_trust_lockfile_left` (stateless v5; known non-bug).
- Offline frozen installs of peer fixtures failed with `ERR_PNPM_NO_OFFLINE_TARBALL` because of an empty store (fixture error). They pass online with an empty store.
