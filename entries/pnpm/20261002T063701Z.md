### False positives ruled out
- Agent apply overwriting a pnpm `patchedDependencies` edit to the same file is the documented default mismatch policy (`content_mismatch_overwritten`; `--strict` refuses instead).
- Hosted keeping an explicit `trustLockfile: false` is documented, and warned.
- pnpm 9 rejects a `pnpm-workspace.yaml` without `packages:` ("packages field missing or empty"). That's a fixture issue.
- `rollback` emptying the staged manifest and GC-ing blobs makes a re-`vendor` a silent no-op (`events: []`). That's expected: there's nothing left to vendor.
