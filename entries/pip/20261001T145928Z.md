## False positives ruled out

- Hosted rollback refusing "mixes hashed and unhashed requirements": my fixture had a hashed `idna` next to unhashed `six`, which pip itself rejects. That was a fixture error.
- Local `rollback -g` failing with "Before blob could not be downloaded": my mock had no `/patches/blob/` route. Once I added it, the cell passed.
- `record_fetch_failed` on probe-1 cells: that mock had no `view` route, so this is a mock artifact.
- `--vendor-source build` was removed in v5, so vendoring needs a patch-service artifact. That's expected, not a bug.
