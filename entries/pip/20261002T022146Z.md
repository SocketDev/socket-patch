## False positives ruled out

- Hosted `rollback` "Cannot restore … GET https://pypi.org/pypi/six/1.16.0/json: error sending request": a sandbox artifact. pypi.org is in `NO_PROXY` and the CLI's direct connection fails here; with `NO_PROXY=localhost,127.0.0.1` it restores correctly.
- Hosted `rollback` "Manifest not found" on a mock-hosted file: you need `--patch-server-url <mock origin>` (already known).
- Lock-only cells with six 1.16.0: confounded by Debian's global six 1.16.0 (the open no-venv fallback question). I switched to 1.17.0 locally and 1.15.0 on the probes.
- The `SIGINT` interruption test was invalid: a non-interactive shell's background jobs ignore SIGINT. I used SIGTERM instead.
