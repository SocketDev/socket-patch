## Ruled out / not filed
- `scan -g` "finding" six in every run: an artifact of the mock, whose `/patches/batch` answers six for any purl. `scannedPackages` and the apply result are the real signal.
- Debian's apt `python3-six` (`six-1.16.0.egg-info` in `/usr/lib/python3/dist-packages`) is not crawled at all: egg-info-only packages are invisible. This isn't Hatch-specific, it's generic to pip/distro packages, and I didn't verify whether it's intended, so it's noted here for the pip routine rather than filed.
- `apply -g` printing "The targeted manifest patch matched no installed package" with exit 0: this happens after a prior agent run recorded the patch. Not investigated further.
