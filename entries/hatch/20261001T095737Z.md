## Filed
- #454: `scan --mode agent` and `scan --sync` never re-apply a patch already recorded in `.socket/manifest.json`. After `hatch env remove` + `hatch env create` (or any reinstall), the re-scan gives `success`, `skipped: 1`, `applied: 0`, exit 0, and six imports unpatched. The `--sync` help promises "fully reconciled in one invocation". Reproduced on Hatch 1.7.0 and 1.18.1 and with `--global-prefix`; released v4.0.0 is the same. Suspect: `get.rs:2438` (the nested apply only runs when `downloaded > 0`). PM-agnostic.
