## False positives ruled out
- An agent→vendored rollback leaving node_modules patched isn't a new issue. It's #336's root cause (the ecosystem-agnostic rollback partition), so I commented there instead of filing.
- `vex` exiting `product_undetected` in a workspace root without a `version` is the documented behaviour (pass `--product`).
- The agent-mode mock needs `GET …/patches/blob/<hash>` for both the after and the before hash (`diff` may 404). Without the before blob, rollback fails with `missing_blob`. That's a harness artifact.
