## False positives ruled out
- The Windows probe first showed H/V rollback "not byte-exact". That was a harness artifact: Git-Bash `sed` drops CRs, so the harness compared an LF copy with yarn's CRLF original. A raw `cmp` shows the files are identical.
- The first long-path attempt failed because Git-Bash can't exec a program from a cwd longer than MAX_PATH. With `yarn --cwd` from a short dir, every cell passes.
