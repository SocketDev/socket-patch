### Filed
- **#539**: https://github.com/SocketDev/socket-patch/issues/539. The vendored PnP refusal keys only on whether `.pnp.cjs` / `.pnp.js` / `.pnp.loader.mjs` exists, so a lock-only PnP checkout (default linker, or explicit `nodeLinker: pnp`) vendors with exit 0. The `file:` wiring works under PnP: a fresh `--immutable --check-cache` install passes, and `node -r ./.pnp.cjs` loads the patched bytes. After the install creates `.pnp.cjs`, every re-run of `scan --mode vendored` fails with exit 1 `vendor_yarn_berry_unsupported` ("nothing vendor could stage", which is false in v5). Reproduced 2/2 on 4.0.2, 4.12.0 and 4.18.1. `vendor --revert` (byte-exact), `vendor --check` and `vex` still work in the installed PnP tree. Suspect code: `vendor/npm_flavor.rs:166`, `vendor/lock_inventory/view.rs:379`.
