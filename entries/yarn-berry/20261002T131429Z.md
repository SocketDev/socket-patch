### Filed
- **#559**: https://github.com/SocketDev/socket-patch/issues/559. `remove <purl>` GC sweeps the beforeHash blobs of every other patch still in the manifest. It pins only not-installed removed entries (`remove.rs:946`), while a scoped `rollback` pins every remaining entry (`rollback.rs:1680`, CLI_CONTRACT:829). A later offline rollback of a still-active patch then fails `missing_blob` and the package stays patched. Reproduced on main with yarn 4.0.2, 4.12.0 and 4.18.1, in agent and v5 manifest-mode vendor, and on releases 4.0.0 and 3.3.0. Not a regression (#235). Cross-PM GC; filed here because it was found and proven in a berry project.
