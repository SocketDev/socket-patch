## False positives ruled out
- Agent `rollback` exit 1 "Before blob could not be downloaded": the mock had no `/patch/blob/<hash>` route. With the route added, rollback passes.
- `remove` exit 1 "No patch found" after a bare `rollback`: v5 rollback removes the entries from the manifest (CLI_CONTRACT), so this is expected.
- A fresh `pipenv sync` failed after the BOM + CRLF takeover, and Pipfile.lock was renamed to `Pipfile.lock.bak`. Pipenv 2026.9.1 does the same with a plain BOM lock that socket-patch never touched (`No matching distribution found for six====1.16.0`). That's Pipenv's own BOM handling, not a socket-patch bug. Added to Known non-bugs.
