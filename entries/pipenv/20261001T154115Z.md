## False positives ruled out
- `--package idna` still redirecting six, and `--min-severity high` filtering a `high` patch as `unknown < high`: both were mock artifacts. The mock ignored the batch body and served `vulnerabilities: {}` on `by-package`.
- The Pipenv 2022.12.19 `pkgutil.ImpImporter` crash: Pipenv 2022 with `PIPENV_PYTHON` built a py3.12 venv under a `-python3.10` name. Use `pipenv --python <3.10>` instead. That's a sandbox and old-Pipenv issue.
