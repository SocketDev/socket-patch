- **Hosted sdist artifact**: **pass** on 2026.9.1 under `resolver_backend = "pip"` and `"pure-python"`, and on 2022.12.19 and 2023.12.1. Each one writes the `file` entry `…/six-1.16.0.tar.gz#sha256=…` plus `hashes`. In a lock-only clone, `pipenv sync` and a fresh `install --deploy` both give PATCHED, and vex gives `not_affected`. When the hash is tampered, `sync` refuses with pip's "DO NOT MATCH THE HASHES". On 2026.9.1, `pipenv install idna==3.7` relocks and keeps the hosted pin under both backends. `rollback` restores the six registry entry equal to the pre-scan entry on 2026.9.1, and the whole lock byte-exact on 2022 and 2023.
- **Interrupted `vendor --revert` and `remove`, using strace `inject=…:signal=KILL` at the Nth rename / unlinkat / unlink / rmdir / write** (a timed kill can't land, because revert takes about 4 ms): **pass**. This covered 18 points on a plain project and 10 × 2 commands on a `use_pylock` project. The only leftover is the already-known `.socket-stage-*` orphan (Known non-bugs, 2026-10-15). - **#1050 `--prune` GC on LF, CRLF, BOM and CRLF+BOM Pipfile.lock** (idna dropped from the Pipfile and the lock, and uninstalled): **pass**. idna is reverted, the six dir is kept and `vendor --check` exits 0. ## False positives ruled out
- After a revert, `vendor_pypi_reinstall_required` says "patched build still installed" while the venv holds unpatched bytes. This is already in Known non-bugs (unconditional by design, #477).
- `scan --prune` exits 1 with `pypi_pipenv_lock_package_missing` when a package removed from the lock by hand is still installed in the venv. That is a setup artifact and a reasonable refusal ("run `pipenv lock` first"). With a real uninstall, the scan passes.
- Pipenv 2026.9.1 writes pylock.toml `wheels` with synthetic `https://pypi.org/simple/<name>/<file>` URLs and a duplicated wheel name. That is Pipenv's own output, so it isn't socket-patch's concern.
