## False positives ruled out
- Under a mirror, a pin on a custom `--patch-server-url` origin isn't moved (`redirect_mirror_pin_not_moved`). That's by design: only patch.socket.dev or the grant origin is owned.
- `use_pylock` rollback writes `{ name = … }` spacing. It's TOML-equivalent and already a known non-bug.
- `uv pip install -r pylock.toml` of a Pipenv pylock installs nothing even before the scan (Pipenv/uv interplay).
