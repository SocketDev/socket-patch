## False positives ruled out
- `rollback` on a project with nothing patched exits 1 "Manifest not found". That's documented (CLI_CONTRACT "truly-empty project").
- Vendored drops the entry's `"index"` key: harmless for a local file, and rollback restores it.
- `vendor_prebuilt_required` / "patch has no applicable files" were mock-API shape gaps (the artifact needs `sha512`, and the view needs `files`), not CLI bugs.
