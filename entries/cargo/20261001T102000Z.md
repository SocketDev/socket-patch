## False positives ruled out

- `repair` (and its GC) deletes the local beforeHash blobs, so a later `rollback --offline` fails with "Before blob not found … --offline prevents fetching". That's by design: `cleanup_unused_blobs` keeps only afterHash blobs, and before-blobs are downloaded on demand during rollback. The failure is loud. Not cargo-specific.
- The `vendor_service_offline_conflict` refusal for `vendor --offline` with no committed artifact is v5 behaviour, documented in `vendor --help`.
