## False positives ruled out

- On Poetry < 1.4, `pypi_poetry_integrity_unverified` is a deliberate advisory (1.x doesn't verify local wheel hashes).
- On Poetry 1.1 / 1.2, `poetry check --lock` fails: that Poetry has no `--lock` option (1.2) or crashes in `check` (1.1). It's not caused by the rewrite, and `poetry install` succeeds.
- Running from a subdirectory is documented as cwd-only.

## Blocked / environment

- A typo in one of my commands replaced `/dev/null` in the sandbox. Restoring it was refused by the sandbox's permission policy, so late Poetry 2.x runs (virtualenv creation, `lock`) failed with `[Errno 2] /dev/null`. Those cells are blocked, not failures.
- No probe branches this run: deleting a branch (`git push --delete`) is refused by permission policy, as in run 1. Creating branches that can't be deleted would leave litter. The two stale probe branches from run 1 still need a maintainer to delete them.
