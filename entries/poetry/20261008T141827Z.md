## False positives ruled out

- The hosted supersede installs first failed with "Hash … not found in known hashes". That was my mock's fault: it rebuilt the wheel per request with fresh zip timestamps, and Poetry then served the stale bytes from its URL-keyed `~/.cache/pypoetry/artifacts`. After fixing the mock and clearing the cache, all hosted cells pass.
- Hosted rollback folds an exported `requirements.txt`'s `\` continuation lines into one line. The content is semantically equal, the file is pip's (not Poetry's), and it's cosmetic, so not filed.
