## False positives ruled out

- Poetry ≤ 2.2 creates the project env during `poetry lock`, so a "fresh checkout" cell must delete the env after locking. Without that, the scan correctly finds the env (that's what the first 1.8.5 / 2.2.1 attempt measured).
- `create = false`: the global fallback is the intended target, so I excluded it from #1023. The over-broad crawl of other interpreters' dirs stays a lead (backlog 1).
