## False positives ruled out

- `rollback` exit 1 (`vendor_revert_kept`) after a hosted → vendored takeover. My harness had copied a "fresh" checkout *inside* the project, and its `poetry.lock` still pointed at the vendored wheels, so the residual-reference guard was right. With the copy outside the project, rollback exits 0.
- A re-vendor dry run "verified with the new uuid": the event carries `oldUuid`, as the e2e test asserts.
- A hosted re-scan reporting `applied: 1` with `rewrittenFiles: []` is the established idempotent shape (explicit `--mode hosted` does the same).
