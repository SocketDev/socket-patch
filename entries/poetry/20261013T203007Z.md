## Issues

- Filed **#1503**: the source-pin guard is keyed on the lock's writer, so a lock-version 1.1 `poetry.lock` (written by Poetry 1.1 / 1.2) with `six = {source = "pypi"}` is rewritten by hosted and vendored with exit 0. Every Poetry 2.x installer (2.0.1 – 2.5.1) then fails with `Repository "pypi" does not exist.`, and the advisory says "2.3+ install it". Root cause: Poetry 2.x re-solves any lock older than format 2.1 (`Locker.is_locked_groups_and_markers`). https://github.com/SocketDev/socket-patch/issues/1503

## False positives ruled out

- Poetry 1.0.10 can't lock a `source = "pypi"` dependency at all, so lock format 1.0 isn't a case for #1503.
- CRLF rollbacks first compared against the LF original and differed. Against the CRLF original they are byte-identical.
- A vendored scan killed partway leaves `.socket/apply.lock` and partial wheels with the lock untouched. The next scan proceeds and converges.
