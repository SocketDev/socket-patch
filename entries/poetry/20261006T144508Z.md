## False positives ruled out

- A vendored takeover that fails with `vendor_prebuilt_required` ("tarball artifact has no sha512 integrity") after un-hosting: my mock grant lacked `sha512`. That's a mock artifact, and real grants carry it.
- 1.1.15 "No dependencies to install" after hosted: a harness artifact. My base project's copied `.venv` had pip entry scripts pointing back at the base. It passes with fresh venvs.
- uv's standalone CPython ships an `EXTERNALLY-MANAGED` marker, so `poetry install` with `create = false` fails in `pip uninstall`. That's a sandbox quirk; delete the marker.
- `redirect_takeover_unpatched` on a refused lock is documented in CLI_CONTRACT.md. #945 is filed for the **dry-run mismatch** and the missing pre-revert gate (requirements.txt has one), not for the warning.
