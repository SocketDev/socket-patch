## Issues

- Filed **#1527**: `vex/product.rs::parse_pyproject` needs `name` and `version` from the same section. Poetry 2's documented dynamic-version layout keeps `name` in `[project]` and `version` in `[tool.poetry]`, so `vex` exits 2 and `scan --vex` exits 1 with `product_undetected`, after writing the hosted pin. This isn't a duplicate of #642, which is about the missing `[tool.pdm]` fallback. https://github.com/SocketDev/socket-patch/issues/1527

## False positives ruled out

- An early "scan patched the global six" came from a broken test `pyproject.toml` (invalid TOML from my harness), so the project wasn't recognised as Poetry. Not a product bug. Restored with `rollback -g`.
- The source-pin guard already reads `dev-dependencies` and every `group.*.dependencies` (unit-tested), so a group-only `source = "pypi"` pin isn't a gap.
