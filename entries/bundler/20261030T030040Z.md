**Ruled out**
- The stale-install guard flags a platform-suffixed install dir after a hosted redirect to the ruby-platform patched gem, and same-run `--vex` exits 1. On 2.6.9 and 4.0.23 the guard is right: `bundle install` keeps the installed platform copy and `bundle exec` prints `VULNERABLE`. On 2.2–2.5 Bundler fetches the patched ruby gem, so the warning is only conservative there. The guard can't know which Bundler will run, so I added this to Known non-bugs rather than filing it.
- `vendor` re-run after a wet eject reports `noManifest` (exit 0). That's the documented manifest-less no-op; the ledger keeps the entry.
