**Ruled out**
- `rollback` / `remove` refuse a CHECKSUMS lock whose upstream remote equals `SOCKET_RUBYGEMS_URL` but isn't rubygems.org. That variable only sets where rubygems.org's sha comes from; the e2e suites spell the upstream as rubygems.org on purpose. `rubygems_locked` (`upstream/gem.rs:1480`) accepts the stand-in, but it only gates the stale-cache warning. Added to Known non-bugs.
- `manifest_not_found` from `rollback` / `remove` on a hosted-only project: this was my harness. Without `--patch-server-url`, a 127.0.0.1 pin isn't a hosted pin (documented, "Patch hosts").
