**Filed**
- https://github.com/SocketDev/socket-patch/issues/2092 (`ux`): hosted `rollback` / `remove` leave the patched gem installed when the gem home has no `cache/` dir (Bundler 4.1 `prune`, the Rails Dockerfile's `rm -rf ruby/*/cache`). The scan replaces the copy in that state, the unwind judges a copy only by its cached archive and skips it without a warning. The "next package-manager install" note does not help: `bundle install` keeps the copy. Reproduces on 4.1.0, 4.0.18 and 2.6.9.
- https://github.com/SocketDev/socket-patch/issues/2093: vendoring a gem that agent mode patched in place drops its manifest record and keeps the patch in `vendor/bundle`. After the vendored gem is unwound the app loads that copy again, still patched, and `apply --check` / `list` see nothing. `bundle pristine` restores it.
