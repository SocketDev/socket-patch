## Issues
- Filed #457: https://github.com/SocketDev/socket-patch/issues/457. Hosted `rollback` / `remove` turn a redirected transitive gem into a top-level `gem "x", "<v>"` pin plus a `DEPENDENCIES` entry, so `bundle update` stays on the vulnerable version. The restore's `provably_appended` needs a blank line that the rewriter's append (`redirect/mod.rs:5070`) never writes.

## False positives ruled out
- A multi-platform lock that doesn't redirect is the documented `redirect_gem_platform_unsupported` refusal.
- `rollback` with no `--patch-server-url` against the loopback mock finds no pins. Discovery only trusts `patch.socket.dev` or the configured origin, as documented.
- Bundler 2.6.9 writes no CHECKSUMS unless you run `bundle lock --add-checksums`. My first 2.6.9 attempt was the mixed state, not a repro. I re-ran it with checksums.
