## False positives ruled out
- Gem dir names that contain `-<digits>.<…>` (`citus-rails-4.2`, `cvss-to-3.1`, and 30 more out of 197k names on rubygems.org) are misparsed by `parse_dir_name_version`'s last-dotted-boundary rule. That's 32 obscure gems with no plausible patches, so it isn't filed.
- Bundler 4.1's new `override` Gemfile DSL can't silently unpatch a hosted pin: the pinned `source … do` block only serves the patched version, so any conflicting override fails loudly. It also isn't counted as a declaration (`declaration_re` needs `gem`).
- Bundler 4.1 content-addressed gems (`gems/<name>-<ver>-<hex8+>`, `CONTENT ADDRESSES` lock section) need RubyGems 4.1 and gems published that way. `parse_dir_name_version` / `locate_gem_dir` already drop the hex suffix like a platform. Untested end to end.
