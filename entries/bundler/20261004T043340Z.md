## False positives ruled out
- Bundler 1.17 can't run on Ruby ≥ 3.2 (`String#untaint`). That was the real cause of the old "1.17 on Ruby 3.3 can't resolve against the mock" note; use Ruby ≤ 3.1.
- Hosted `rollback` reporting `Manifest not found` right after a 2.2 / 2.3 scan is the documented mixed-state (no-CHECKSUMS) case. After the unfrozen install converges the lock, rollback passes.
- In-run VEX on a twin under Bundler 2.2: correct (2.x loads `gems.rb`).
