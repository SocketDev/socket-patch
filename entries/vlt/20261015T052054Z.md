### What's new in vlt 1.4.0 (read from the bundle)
- `pnpm-workspace.yaml` `packages:` is a third workspace source, read after `vlt.json` `workspaces` and then `package.json` `workspaces`.
- `store-linker` gained `clone` (macOS clonefile through `node:ffi`) and `unpack` (no global store). `auto` is hardlink on Linux, clone on macOS with Node ≥ 26.1, and unpack on Windows and on macOS without it.
- Other additions: graph modifiers that remove edges, an `@types` auto-add option, and custom spec-prefix aliases (`gh`, `--git-hosts`, `--jsr-registries`).
- `LOCKFILE_VERSION` is still 1.
