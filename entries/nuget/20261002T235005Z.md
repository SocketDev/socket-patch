## False positives ruled out
- Hosted `remove` / `rollback` giving `manifest_not_found` on a manifest-less hosted project was a harness artifact: lockfile discovery only recognises production `https://patch.socket.dev` feed URLs, and the stand-in writes `http://127.0.0.1`. After a `sed` to the production URL it gives `hosted_reverted`. Always rewrite the URL before unwind probes.
- Version bump after vendoring/hosting (the exclusive id mapping routes only to a feed holding the patched version, so NU1102): a design limitation of id-level `packageSourceMapping`, not filed.
